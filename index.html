How Kenyan pay is taxed.

The deductions on a Kenyan payslip, at the rates in force for the 2025/26 tax year. Always confirm current figures with KRA, NSSF and the SHA before relying on them for payroll.

PAYE · income tax

Progressive tax bands

Applied to your taxable income each month. Only the slice of pay inside each band is taxed at that band's rate — not your whole salary.

First KES 24,000up to 24,000 10%
Next KES 8,33324,001 – 32,333 25%

Personal relief: every resident gets KES 2,400/month (KES 28,800/year) knocked off the tax due. The 32.5% and 35% top bands came in under the Finance Act 2023.

Finance Act · the yearly picture

What the Finance Bill changes

Each year a Finance Bill goes through Parliament and, once assented to, becomes the Finance Act that sets the rates above. The current PAYE bands trace to the Finance Act 2023; SHIF arrived with the Social Health Insurance Act 2023, the housing levy with the Affordable Housing Act 2024, and NSSF limits step up in phases under the NSSF Act 2013. Because these shift most years, treat this page as a snapshot and confirm before you run live payroll.
